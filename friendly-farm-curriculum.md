Book map on pp. 2-3 checked for titles, grammar and projects. 207 vocabulary entries across units (includes repeated words and multi-word phrases), 60 grammar items, 80 selectable missions. Each complete mission selection generates 597 prompts including 30 parent-confirmed real-life tasks; read/spelling selections vary between plays.

Core/extension listening missions cover both included vocabulary banks. Reading and spelling sample the bank each play. Grammar examples, dialogue choices, knowledge questions, short stories and real-life prompts are original adaptations. The included stories do not reproduce the textbook stories. This is not an exhaustive transcription of the book, not official Cambridge software, and not an official exam.

All playback currently uses browser/device speech synthesis; no textbook audio or ElevenLabs recordings are bundled. Speech preferences and progress are browser-local. Original v1 progress remains stored; new mission awards are tracked separately. Assisted answers stay in review until answered independently. Oral activities record parent confirmation and award no automated proficiency score.

Validation: JS syntax; all 80 banks and valid choices; cloze reconstruction; old-state migration; review lifecycle; score awards; spelling interaction. Browser verified U1 menu, grammar retry/next, and mobile layout.
